# 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-15

## 简体中文

口袋软盘不收集你的任何个人数据。

- **没有账号，没有广告，没有统计。** App 里没有登录、广告或用量统计，也没有任何第三方数据收集组件。
- **你的文件留在你的设备上。** 导入的游戏、存档、截图、便利贴和设置都只保存在你的设备上（以及你自己的备份里）。App 不会把它们上传到任何服务器。
- **游戏离线运行。** 你导入的游戏在 App 里离线运行。
- **翻译在设备上完成。** “翻译画面”“实时翻译”和游戏内翻译使用系统自带的文字识别和翻译功能，文字不会离开设备。第一次使用某种语言时，系统可能会向 Apple 下载语言包，这由系统完成。
- **Wi‑Fi 传输只在你打开时接收文件。** “从电脑用 Wi‑Fi 传过来”页面打开期间，App 在本地网络上接收你从电脑发来的文件；它只接收，不向外发送，关掉页面就停止。
- **购买由 Apple 处理。** App 内购买通过 App Store 完成，开发者拿不到你的付款信息。
- **诊断日志由你决定是否分享。** 只有当你自己点“导出诊断日志”并把文件发出去时，日志才会离开设备。日志里是游戏运行的技术记录。

如果这份政策有变化，会更新在本页面。有问题可以在这个仓库的 Issues 里提出。

## English

Pocket Floppy does not collect any personal data.

- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics.** The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no usage statistics and no third-party data collection components.
- **Your files stay on your device.** Imported games, saves, screenshots, notes and settings are kept only on your device (and in your own backups). The app does not upload them anywhere.
- **Games run offline.** The games you import run offline inside the app.
- **Translation happens on the device.** "Translate the screen", "Live translation" and in-game translation use the system's own text recognition and translation; the text does not leave the device. The first time a language is used, the system may download it from Apple.
- **Wi‑Fi transfer only receives files while you have it open.** While the "Send from a computer over Wi‑Fi" page is open, the app receives the files you send from your computer over the local network; it only receives, sends nothing out, and stops when the page is closed.
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** In-app purchases go through the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details.
- **Diagnostic logs are shared only if you choose to.** A log leaves the device only when you tap "Export diagnostic log" and send the file yourself. It holds technical records of how a game ran.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised in this repository's Issues.
